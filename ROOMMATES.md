# 🏠 Bell House — Roommate Setup Guide

Welcome to **Bell House**, the app we use to track chores, supplies, trash pickup days, and notes for 295 Bell Ave. Setup takes under 2 minutes.

---

## ✅ Quick start (TL;DR)

1. Open the app link Mandy sent you.
2. **Add it to your home screen** (instructions below per phone).
3. Open it from the **home-screen icon** (not Safari/Chrome).
4. Tap your name in the "Who are you?" picker.
5. Tap **Allow** when it asks about notifications.

You're in. Read the rest if you want the full tour.

---

## 📱 Install on iPhone (required for notifications)

**You must install it as an app** — Safari tabs don't get push notifications on iPhone. Apple's rule, not ours.

1. Open the link Mandy sent you in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down → tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right.
5. Find the new "Bell House" icon on your home screen and tap it from there.
6. Sign in by tapping your name.
7. When iOS asks "Bell House Would Like to Send You Notifications," tap **Allow**.

**Requires iOS 16.4 or newer.** Check at Settings → General → About → iOS Version. If you're older, update first.

---

## 🤖 Install on Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Chrome will usually show a banner: "Add Bell House to Home screen" — tap it. If you don't see it: tap the ⋮ menu (top-right) → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm **Install**.
4. Open the app from the new home-screen icon.
5. Sign in by tapping your name.
6. Tap **Allow** when Chrome asks about notifications.

## 👤 Pick your name (first time only)

The first time you open the app, you'll see a **"Who are you?"** screen with one button per roommate.

- Tap your name.
- The app remembers you on this device. You won't have to do this again unless you reinstall.
- To switch users later (e.g. someone else borrows your phone), tap the **chip with your name** in the top-right corner.

---

## 🔔 Notifications

When someone nudges you about a chore, your phone buzzes and a notification shows on the lock screen / banner — even when the app is closed. To make sure that works:

- **iPhone**: Settings → Notifications → **Bell House** → make sure **Allow Notifications** is ON. Pick banner style + sound under the same screen.
- **Android**: Settings → Apps → Bell House → Notifications → ON.

If you didn't get the permission prompt the first time, uninstall the app from your home screen, reinstall it, and grant permission when asked.

## 🗂️ The tabs, in 30 seconds

- **Home** — today at a glance. Pending chores, what's running low, upcoming trash/recycling, your personal dashboard.
- **Chores** — every chore in the house. Tap the circle to mark done. Tap ✏️ to edit (frequency, day, who it's assigned to). Filter by roommate using the chips at the top.
- **Trash** — when trash and recycling go out. Tap any pickup card to edit days or change the type tag (Paper / Plastic, Cans, Glass).
- **Calendar** — month view with dots for chores, events, and trash days. Tap any day for details + the recent **Activity** log (who did what).
- **Board** — pinned house announcements. Use it for "AC is out, repair guy coming Tuesday" type stuff.
- **Notes** — house notes + the **🛒 Low Stock** list (more on that below).

## 🧹 Common things you'll do

### Mark a chore done
Tap the **empty circle** next to it. Done. The chore turns gray, the activity log records you did it, and if it's a rotating chore the rotation advances.

Tapped by accident? Tap the green circle again to un-mark it.

### Nudge someone
On the Chores tab or Home, find their chore card → tap **👋 Nudge**. Their phone buzzes within a few seconds.

Nudges only fire for chores that aren't yours. Be nice.

### Add something to the low-stock list
Notes tab → **🛒 Low Stock** card → **+ Add** → type the item (e.g. "Toilet Paper") → optional emoji → **Add**.

When someone restocks it, they tap **✓ Got it** on that row and it disappears.

### Edit a chore
Chores tab → tap the **✏️** on the chore → change name, frequency, day, or who it's assigned to. **Auto Rotate** cycles between roommates each time it's marked done; uncheck it and tick a specific person if you want it permanently assigned.

### Add an event to the calendar
Calendar tab → tap the day → **+ Add** → name, optional notes → **Add Event**.

### Edit recycling type (Paper vs Plastic/Cans/Glass)
Trash tab → tap the Recycling card → set tags (multiple tags cycle each pickup) → set the **Anchor date** to the Wednesday you want the first tag to apply → Save.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**My changes aren't saving / "Save failed" toast.**
Force-reload the app first: iPhone — swipe the app off the app switcher and reopen from the home-screen icon. Android — long-press the icon → App info → Storage → Clear storage, then reopen. If it persists, ping Mandy — likely a Firebase issue she can fix in 30 seconds.

**I'm not getting notifications when someone nudges me.**
Check, in order:
1. You opened the app from the **home-screen icon**, not Safari/Chrome tab.
2. Your iOS version is **16.4 or newer**.
3. **Allow Notifications** is on in Settings → Notifications → Bell House.
4. You signed in (tapped your name) inside the installed app at least once after installing.

**The app says "Who are you?" every time.**
You're opening it from the Safari tab instead of the home-screen icon. They're different storage contexts. Use the icon.

**Something's wrong with the chore rotation order / it skipped me.**
Anyone can fix it: Chores tab → ✏️ → reorder with the up/down arrows in the rotation section → Save.
